AIvidea Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

AIvidea Studio ("the tool") is a private tool used by its owner to publish videos to YouTube channels that the owner controls. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the tool accesses

  • Basic information about the authorized YouTube channel (channel ID, title).
  • Statistics of the channel and of videos uploaded by the tool (views, likes, comments, subscriber count).
  • The ability to upload videos and set their metadata and thumbnails on the authorized channel.

The tool does not access data of any other YouTube user.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to show the owner a private performance dashboard. OAuth tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept as long as the owner keeps the local database and are deleted when it is deleted. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings, after which the tool can no longer access the channel.

Cookies

The tool does not use cookies or tracking technologies.
